### Discord Server Announcement
> **Shhh Printer v1.0.0 is here!**
> 
> Looking for an automated building assistant that works seamlessly with your schematics without acting like an unfair cheat mod? **Shhh Printer** is the official companion mod for **Shhh Builder** on Fabric 26.3.
> 
> Key Highlights:
> - Point & Place Mode: Rather than placing blocks automatically 360 degrees around you, Shhh Printer only places the block your crosshair is pointing at.
> - Anti-Cheat Safe Defaults: Air placement is disabled by default, requiring valid adjacent block support just like vanilla building.
> - Presets: Switch between Survival (10 blk/s), Public Server (5 blk/s safe cadence), and Creative with one click.
> - Inventory Management: Automatically picks items from your hotbar or swaps from main inventory.
> - Client-Only: No server plugins or special permissions required.
> 
> CurseForge: https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/shhh-printer
> Modrinth: https://modrinth.com/mod/shhh-printer
> Companion Mod: https://github.com/rafayshhh/shhh-builder
